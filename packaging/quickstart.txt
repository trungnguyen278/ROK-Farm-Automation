ROK FARM -- BAT DAU O DAY
=========================

1. Nhay CHUOT PHAI vao 'ROK Farm.exe' -> Run as administrator
2. Cua so co 4 the. Lam tu trai sang phai:

     The 1 - Mach ESP32 : bam 'Nap firmware vao mach'
     The 2 - Chay farm  : bam 'Bat dau farm'
     The 3 - Discord    : dan token + ID, de dieu khien tu dien thoai
     The 4 - Thong ke   : so mo da farm, ti le thanh cong


QUAN TRONG -- mach ESP32-S3 co HAI cong USB-C
---------------------------------------------

     Luc NAP  : chi can cong UART/COM
     Luc CHAY : phai cam CA HAI cong vao may tinh.

Thieu cong thu hai thi mach van tra loi binh thuong nhung con tro chuot
KHONG he nhuc nhich -- day la loi kho doan nhat, vi moi thu trong nhu
dang chay dung.


CAP USB
-------

Phai dung cap TRUYEN DU LIEU. Cap chi-de-sac trong y het nhung khong chay.
Neu app bao khong thay mach, viec DAU TIEN la doi cap khac.


LOAI MACH
---------

Can ESP32-S3 DevKitC-1, ban N16R8 (16MB flash).
ESP32 thuong (khong phai S3) KHONG chay duoc: no khong co USB goc nen
khong gia lam chuot duoc.
